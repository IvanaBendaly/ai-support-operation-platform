# Northstar Cloud refund policy

## Eligibility
A paid invoice may be refunded within 30 calendar days of issuance, including day 30.
The invoice must not have been refunded already. Future-dated and unpaid invoices
are not eligible.

## Amount and review
Automatic refunds are full-invoice refunds in USD, up to 10,000 minor units ($100).
Larger requests require human review. Multiple candidate invoices require invoice
selection before action; do not infer the customer's intended invoice.

## Account status
Suspended accounts require review before automated business changes. This document
is a fictional development fixture, not a real commercial policy.
